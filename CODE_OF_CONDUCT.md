# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of LAB AI OS, we pledge to respect and support one another. We are committed to creating a welcoming and inclusive community for contributors of all backgrounds and identities.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, or abusive language
- Discriminatory or hateful language based on identity
- Public or private insults, personal attacks, or threats
- Posting private or personally identifying information without consent

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at conduct@labaios.org. Reports will be handled promptly and with discretion.

Maintainers who are uncertain about the appropriate action should reach out to the project governance group or advisors.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces where an individual represents the project.
